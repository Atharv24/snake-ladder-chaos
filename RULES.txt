SNAKES, LADDERS AND CHAOS!  -  RULES AND CLASS ABILITIES
=========================================================

THE BASICS
----------
* 2 to 6 players. Every player picks a different class. (If two players pick the same class they roll a die; the higher roll keeps it
  and the other picks again.)
* The board has 100 squares, numbered in a zig-zag (1 bottom-left, 100 top-left). Everyone starts on square 1.
* Every game has a random board: 9 snakes (one of them is a long cobra snake) and 9 ladders.
* Landing on the BOTTOM of a ladder takes you up to its top. Landing on a snake's HEAD takes you down to its tail.
* A turn is: (1) use items if you want, (2) roll, (3) drag your pawn to the glowing target square. (Press "Move for me" or Space instead
  of dragging if you prefer.) The roll can be thrown by grabbing the die, or with the ROLL button / Space bar.
* You win by landing on square 100 EXACTLY. If your roll would take you past 100 you stay where you are
  (the Gambler is the one exception, see below).
* Rolling a 6 gives you another turn - EXCEPT where a class says otherwise (see the table below).
* Turn order: the Snake Charmer always goes first, the owner of the Cat always goes last, everyone else goes in the order they were
  added on the start screen.
* BUFF and DEBUFF counters (under each player's panel) show a bonus (+) or penalty (-) that is added to the player's NEXT roll and then
  cleared. Bonuses and penalties add together. A move can never be less than 0 squares (it cannot push you backwards).
* Anyone the game moves (pushed, pulled, nudged, knocked down, slid down a snake, climbed a ladder) takes snakes and ladders at the
  square they land on, just like a normal move - except where a class says otherwise.

BONUS ROLL ON A 6 - WHO GETS IT
-------------------------------
  Bully ................ yes (on a 6)
  Struggler ............ yes (on a 6)
  Oracle ............... yes (on a 6, whether the call was right or wrong)
  Cat owner ............ yes for her own roll; the CAT also gets another roll whenever it rolls a 6
  Gambler .............. NO bonus for a single 6. He only gets another turn when BOTH dice are 6 (double 6s)
  Snake Charmer ........ NEVER gets a bonus roll, not even on a 6

THE CLASSES
-----------

BULLY
* Before rolling: if another player is within 6 squares ahead of him, he chooses PUSH or PULL. If nobody is within 6 squares ahead
  he is not asked (he cannot land on anybody) and just rolls.
* Passing over a player (that player's square is between where he started and where he finishes): for each one he chooses to nudge them
  forward 1 or back 1 square (they cannot go below square 1).
* Landing exactly on another player: he rolls a die and PUSHES that player forward (or PULLS them back) by that many squares, in the
  direction he chose before rolling. If a push would carry them past 100, nothing happens. If two players share the square he picks one.
* His ability triggers where he LANDS with his own roll, before any snake or ladder. If a snake swallows him and he ends up on a
  tail where another player is standing, the ability does NOT go off.
* A 6 gives him another turn.

GAMBLER
* Rolls TWO dice and moves the total (plus/minus any buff/debuff).
* If either die shows a 1, the gamble backfires: he goes BACK by the other die (never below square 1) and his turn ends.
  Double 1s ("snake eyes"): he stays where he is.
* NO bonus roll for a normal 6. He only gets another turn on double 6s.
* If the total would take him past 100 he BOUNCES BACK instead of staying put: he ends on (200 - target). For example from square 95 a
  total of 12 would be 107, so he lands on 93.
* THE FINISH: from square 97, 98 or 99 (3 or fewer squares from 100) the total no longer counts. He needs EITHER die to show the exact
  number he needs (e.g. on 98 he needs a 2 on either die; on 99 he needs a 1, so a 1 on either die WINS there). If he rolls it, he wins.
  If not, he stays where he is - except that a 1 still sends him back by the other die (double 1s: he stays). Double 6s still give another turn.
  (From 96 and below the normal total-and-bounce rule applies, so he can finish with an exact total of 100.)

THE ONE WITH THE CAT (CAT OWNER)
* Her own turn is a plain roll: a 6 gives another turn. She goes LAST in the turn order.
* After her own turn the CAT takes a turn: she rolls a die for the cat. The cat moves forward that many squares and WRAPS AROUND the
  board (past 100 it carries on from square 1; landing on exactly 100 sends it to square 1). The cat rolls again if it rolls a 6.
* The cat ignores snakes, ladders and items, and cannot win the game. Items cannot be used on the cat and the cat cannot be a target.
* Players the cat JUMPS OVER (squares between where it started and where it lands) are INFATUATED: -1 on their next roll.
* A player the cat LANDS ON is KNOCKED DOWN 10 squares (to square 1 if they were on 10 or below). They slide down a snake or climb a ladder
  if they land on one. For each player actually knocked down the owner gets +3 on her next roll (a Struggler who dodges does not count).
  If that roll overshoots 100 the bonus is lost.
* Click the cat (any player, any time) to change its picture. It also changes whenever the cat moves to a new square.

STRUGGLER
* Plain roll; a 6 gives another turn.
* Every time something forced happens to him he rolls a die: EVEN = he dodges it, ODD = it hits him. This applies to snakes (even = he
  wriggles free and stays on the head square), being pushed/pulled/nudged by the Bully, being knocked down by the cat, and being
  infatuated by the cat.
* LADDERS work the other way round: before climbing he picks TWO failure numbers (1-6), then rolls. If the roll is one of his failure
  numbers he does NOT climb and stays on the bottom square; any other number and he climbs.
* Items work on him normally and NEVER trigger his dodge ability.

ORACLE
* Before rolling she calls a number from 1 to 6.
* If the roll matches her call she rolls a 3-sided bonus die (1-3) and adds it to her move, and snakes cannot bite her on that move
  (ladders still work). A wrong call costs nothing - she just moves normally.
* If the boosted move would carry her past 100 she stays put.
* A 6 gives another turn (right call or wrong).

SNAKE CHARMER
* Always goes FIRST in every round.
* Before rolling she may drag the head of one snake that is on her row, or on the row directly above or below her, to another FREE square on
  that same row. The square must still be above the snake's tail and must not already be used by a snake or a ladder (head, tail, bottom or top). The snake's tail stays
  put; the body follows the head. Players already standing on the new head square are safe - only players who arrive there later get bitten.
* After moving a head she must wait one turn before she can move another ("recharging"). She can always choose "Just roll" instead.
* NO bonus roll on a 6 - she never gets another turn.

ITEMS
-----
* The game starts with 6 random items lying on empty squares. A new item appears on a random empty square every 3rd turn of the game.
  Items never spawn on square 1 or 100, on a snake head or the bottom of a ladder, or on a square that already has an item, a pawn or the cat.
  If an item and a snake head share a tile, the player who lands there picks the item up before the snake bites. The 6 starting items have a tiny bias
  towards the bottom half of the board; items that appear later have a tiny bias towards squares near the player who is furthest from 100.
* Step on an item (any way a pawn ends up on its square) to pick it up. Each player holds up to 3 items in the slots on their panel.
  With full hands you may swap one of your items for the one on the ground, or leave it.
* Items can only be used BEFORE rolling. During your turn your items pulse with a lime glow: click one to use it, or press Roll / Space to
  go on without using any. You can use several items, one after another. When an item asks for a target you can press Cancel / Space to
  back out without spending it.
* Items work on every class, including the Struggler (they never trigger his dodge roll). Nothing can be used on the cat or against it.

  STICK ......... Nudge ANY snake's head 1 square left or right (onto a free square on its row, still above its tail).
  TOY BRICK ..... Place it on any free square in your own row or the row above/below (not under a pawn, the cat, or another item).
                  The next player to step on it - including you - gets -3 on their next roll, and the brick is used up. Placed bricks glow
                  red so they are easy to tell from bricks lying around to be picked up. The cat does not trigger bricks.
  ENERGY DRINK .. +3 on your next roll. Your pawn grows a little for the rest of the turn. (It cannot be given to the cat.)
  STONE ......... Throw it at any other player: -1 on their next roll. (It cannot be thrown at the cat.)

HELPFUL CONTROLS
----------------
* Space bar: roll the dice / press the helper button on the rolling board ("Move for me", "Just roll", "Roll", "Cancel").
* Settings tab: sound-effects volume (10 steps; click step 1 again to mute).
* Rulebook tab: a short version of these rules. Board log: everything that happens, with a bit of flavour text.
